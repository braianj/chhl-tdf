# November 29, 2026 Paper Forms

## Official match scoresheet

There is one landscape page per fixture. It records both lineups, scorers and up to two assists, period and remaining clock, period/OT/final result, goalkeeper minutes/saves/goals against, penalties, shootout rounds, timeouts, and signatures. Empty or unreadable fields stay empty in the application. Shootout attempts do not become player goals or shots on target.

## Shots-on-target control

There is one side per team for each match. A mark means a shot that became a goal or was saved. Missed or blocked-off-target attempts do not count. The digital fields are first period, second period, and overtime; player and team totals are derived.

The team total should equal the opposing goalkeeper total of saves plus goals against. The administration panel reports mismatches but preserves partial data.

## Standings note

The supplied tournament notice defines the tiebreak order as direct-match points, direct-match goal difference, overall goal difference, overall goals for, fewer overall goals against, and finally a draw. The application does not substitute alphabetical order for the draw.

Regular-phase matches may finish tied and award one point to each team. A final cannot finish tied.
